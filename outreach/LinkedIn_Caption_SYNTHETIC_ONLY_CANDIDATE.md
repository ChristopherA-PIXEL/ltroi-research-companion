**A vehicle can fail less often and still cost more to keep in service.**

Imagine 100 comparable vehicles in each of two fleets over the same exposure period. Ten repairs at £300 cost £3,000. Seven repairs at £1,500 cost £10,500. These are invented examples, not manufacturer observations.

Fault frequency matters. So do repair severity, distinct unusable days, substitute transport, recoveries and the repair-versus-replace decision.

LTROI v2.3 proposes a cohort-specific actuarial–economic framework for these questions. Its synthetic implementation includes acquisition, replacement and terminal resale in a restricted service-continuity TCO account. It is not a full vehicle TCO forecast or an empirical LTROI ranking.

The companion lets readers stress-test wear parameters, parts expenditure, downtime and discounting. It remains a local review candidate; no live URL is claimed here.
